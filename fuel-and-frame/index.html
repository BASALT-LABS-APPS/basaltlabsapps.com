Made to keep you going

Most apps only log. Fuel & Frame turns what you log into clear next steps.

Eat with a plan

Log food in seconds by search, barcode or plain words. Targets adjust to your real progress, with calorie cycling for training days and intermittent fasting built in.

Train with purpose

Programs and your own workouts, an Apple Watch player, a readiness check before you train, and clear signs when you're ready to go up in weight.

Stay motivated

Daily rings and streaks, two-week experiments, "Then and now" progress, and your reason for starting — shown on the days you need it most.

Your data stays yours

No ads, no tracking and no selling of data. Apple Health data is never sold or used for marketing, and private things like glucose readings stay on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
