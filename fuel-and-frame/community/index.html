Community Rules

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Friends in Fuel & Frame is for cheering each other on. These rules apply to everything people can see from you: your username, name and picture, the moments and photos you share, challenges, and foods you add to the shared food list.

Not allowed

  • Bullying, threats, harassment, or hate based on who someone is.
  • Nudity, sexual content, or graphic or violent images.
  • Selling, advertising, spam, or links to other services.
  • Medical claims: diet pills, “cures”, or advice to stop treatment.
  • Photos you don't have the right to share, or photos of other people without their permission.
  • Impersonating someone, or pretending to be Fuel & Frame or a coach.
  • Foods with made-up numbers, misleading names, or offensive words.

Zero tolerance

We don't tolerate objectionable content or abusive people. Anything that breaks these rules is removed, and people who post it lose access to Friends and to adding shared foods. Serious or repeated breaks can close the account.

How we keep it safe

  • Words we don't allow are stopped before a caption, username, name or shared food is saved.
  • Report any moment, person or shared food from its menu. It's hidden for you straight away, and the person isn't told who reported it.
  • Three reports hide a moment or shared food for everyone until we've reviewed it.
  • We review every report within 24–48 hours and remove what breaks these rules.
  • Block anyone from a friend request, their profile, or a moment. You're hidden from each other everywhere, and they aren't told.

Contact

Questions or an urgent concern: support@basaltlabsapps.com. If you think something of yours was removed by mistake, email us and we'll look again.
